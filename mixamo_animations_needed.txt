--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: AUTO-RIG IN MIXAMO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://www.mixamo.com and log in (free Adobe account).
2. Click "UPLOAD CHARACTER" on the right panel.
3. Drag and drop "preethi_tpose.fbx" from this folder.
4. In the Auto-Rigger chin/wrists/elbows/knees/groin marker screen:
   - Chin: Center of chin
   - Wrists: Center of wrists
   - Elbows: Center of elbow joints
   - Knees: Center of knee joints
   - Groin: Base of pelvis / groin
   - Skeleton LOD: Standard Skeleton (65 bones, includes fingers)
5. Click Next. When auto-rigging completes, Preethi will be rigged!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: EXACT MIXAMO ANIMATIONS TO SEARCH & DOWNLOAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT: For movement animations (Run, Strafe, etc.), ALWAYS check the 
"In Place" checkbox in the Mixamo right-hand panel before downloading! 
In-game forward translation is controlled by the Three.js / game engine.

Download each animation as:
Format: FBX for Unity (.fbx) or FBX (.fbx)
Skin: "Without Skin" (to keep file sizes small, since we already have the base mesh)
      *Or "With Skin" if you want preview in Windows 3D Viewer.
Frames per Second: 30 or 60 FPS
Keyframe Reduction: None

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE RUNNING (Main Gameplay)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Search in Mixamo: "Running" or "Fast Run"
- Recommended Mixamo Asset: "Running" (or "Standard Run")
- CRITICAL SETTING: Check [x] "In Place" checkbox!
- In-Game Action: Endless running loop across the 3 railway tracks.
- Save As: `anim_run.fbx`

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. JUMPING (Obstacle Avoidance)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Search in Mixamo: "Jump" or "Running Jump"
- Recommended Mixamo Asset: "Running Jump"
- Settings: High arc, clean take-off and landing.
- In-Game Action: Jump over low track barriers, hurdles, and onto train roofs.
- Save As: `anim_jump.fbx`

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SLIDING / DUCKING (Low Clearance Obstacles)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Search in Mixamo: "Running Slide" or "Baseball Slide"
- Recommended Mixamo Asset: "Running Slide"
- Settings: Low body slide under overhead obstacle beams.
- In-Game Action: Swipe down to slide beneath overhead hazard beams.
- Save As: `anim_slide.fbx`

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Search in Mixamo: "Left Strafe" and "Right Strafe"

- CRITICAL SETTING: Check [x] "In Place"
